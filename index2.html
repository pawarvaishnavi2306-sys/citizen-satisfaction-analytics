Decision support · transparent scoring

What may need attention first?

The priority score is a project-defined indicator derived from reported frequency, perceived severity, daily-life impact and how many respondents selected an issue as their main concern. It is not an official civic authority ranking.

Priority board

Higher scores indicate stronger concern within this sample.

Method: 40% average reported frequency, 30% mean severity, 30% mean daily-life impact, normalised to 100. Frequency mapping: Rarely=1, Sometimes=2, Often=3, Very often=4, Daily=5. Not-applicable responses are excluded from mean frequency. Scores are descriptive and depend on this sample size.

How to interpret the score

1

Frequency

How often respondents experience the issue.

2

Severity

How serious respondents say the issue is, rated 1–5.

3

Daily-life impact

How much the issue affects routine life, rated 1–5.
